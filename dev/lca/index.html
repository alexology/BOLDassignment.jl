The LCA assignment

Assignment rule

Two directions are available (direction keyword of lca_path, lca_assign and bold_assign). The default, :top_down, descends from phylum to species; :bottom_up starts from the species and moves up.

Top-down (default)

For each sequence and each rank, from phylum to species:

  • the pool contains the hits with similarity ≥ the rank threshold (thresholds, default as BOLDigger3: phylum 50, class 75, order 85, family 90, genus 95, species 97 %) and ≥ best similarity − top_delta (default Inf, i.e. thresholds only);
  • the most frequent taxon among the pool hits that agree with the ranks already assigned is accepted when it is supported by at least min_agreement percent of the pool (default 100, strict LCA);
  • otherwise, or on a tie for the most frequent taxon, the assignment stops.

top_delta = Inf is a deliberate default: every hit above the rank threshold takes part, so a congener at 97.5 % keeps the species open even when the best hit is at 100 %. This is the conservative choice; a finite value (e.g. top_delta = 1) lets the closest hits decide, closer to top-hit methods such as BOLDigger3. Neither setting handles misidentified records close to the best hit: use min_agreement below 100 for those.

Only hits identified at a rank vote at that rank. A hit whose name is missing or not valid (is_valid_name) leaves the pool at that rank and at the deeper ones. At species level this removes open nomenclature and records identified only to genus or above: Onthophagus sp., Onthophagus sp. 1, Onthophagus cf. taurus, aff., nr., complex, interim codes such as Diptera sp. BIOUG1234-A01. Such a hit above 97 % therefore neither blocks nor supports the species, but still supports the genus. With ignore_missing = false these hits stay in the pool and count against the agreement; valid_name = (rank, name) -> ... replaces the name check. lca_path applies the rule to a single set of hits.

Bottom-up

With direction = :bottom_up the ranks are visited from species to phylum, and the first rank whose pool agrees is the decision rank:

  • the pool of a rank is defined as above (threshold and top_delta);
  • only hits with a valid name at that rank vote; with ignore_missing = true the others leave the pool of that rank, with ignore_missing = false they stay and count against the agreement. Each rank is judged on its own: a record of Protonemura sp. does not vote for the species but still votes for the genus;
  • the most frequent taxon is accepted if it is unique (no tie) and supported by at least min_agreement percent of the pool; otherwise, or with an empty pool, the next more general rank is tried. If no rank agrees, the sequence is not assigned.

The ranks above the decision rank are taken from the supporting hits (the pool hits voting for the accepted taxon): at each rank, the most frequent valid name among them. For these ranks <rank>_agreement is the percent of supporting hits with that name, not a share of the rank's pool. If they disagree (e.g. the same species name under two genera in BOLD), the most frequent name is kept and lineage_conflict is true; on a tie that rank and the more general ones stay missing. With this direction lca_assign and bold_assign add the columns decision_rank and lineage_conflict; with the default direction the columns are unchanged.

Example. Best hit 99.3 % on Protonemura intricata (Plecoptera); 45 hits on Plecoptera between 85.9 and 99.3 %, 55 on Diptera between 85.4 and 86.5 %. Top-down with top_delta = Inf, the order pool (≥ 85 %) contains both orders (45 against 55), so the assignment stops at the class Insecta. Bottom-up, the species pool (≥ 97 %) holds only P. intricata hits, so the species is assigned, and genus, family, order, class and phylum follow from those hits.

Which one to use. Top-down is the strict LCA: a rank is assigned only when all the hits above its threshold agree on every more general rank, so distant hits of other groups can stop the assignment early, as in the example. It suits cautious inventories and incomplete reference libraries, where a close match may still be misleading. Bottom-up asks, for each rank, whether the hits close enough for that rank agree; distant hits do not block a well-supported species. It is closer to how a close match is read by hand and to threshold-based classifiers. In both, a valid congener above the species threshold (e.g. 97.5 % against a best hit of 100 %) blocks the species with min_agreement = 100; bottom-up then assigns the genus. Check lineage_conflict before relying on the higher ranks.
